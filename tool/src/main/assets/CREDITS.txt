Bundled games: Zork I, Zork II and Zork III
=============================================

The three story files in this folder are the prebuilt files from the COMPILED folder of
each repository below. The source code in those repositories was released by Microsoft
(with Activision and Xbox) under the MIT License on November 20, 2025.

  Zork I    https://github.com/historicalsource/zork1   (release 119, serial 880429)
  Zork II   https://github.com/historicalsource/zork2   (release 63,  serial 860811)
  Zork III  https://github.com/historicalsource/zork3   (release 25,  serial 860811)

Original games by Infocom. The Zork I README names Marc Blank, Dave Lebling, Bruce Daniels
and Tim Anderson as its authors.

The MIT License text is in LICENSE-MIT-Microsoft.txt and must stay with these files.

These three games are UNOFFICIAL FAN MODIFICATIONS. They are not Infocom, Activision or

They were built with ZILF from modified copies of the MIT-licensed source in the three

source and does not mean the game is official.
